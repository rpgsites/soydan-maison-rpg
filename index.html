The Maison

SOYDAN MAISON was imagined as more than a fashion house. It is a point of view: a celebration of femininity, confidence, craftsmanship and the art of making an impression without saying a word.

Every silhouette is a statement. Every detail, a signature. The Maison believes elegance is not inherited—it is cultivated, lived and remembered.

A legacy of elegance, crafted to endure.
The vision behind the Maison

Katarzyna Soydan

Founder · Creative Director

With a discerning eye and an instinct for the extraordinary, Katarzyna “Kate” Soydan leads the Maison with uncompromising standards. Her vision is precise, sensual and unapologetically ambitious—where refinement meets risk, and beauty never settles for ordinary.

Her world is built around contrast: discipline and desire, timelessness and adrenaline, restraint and spectacle.
